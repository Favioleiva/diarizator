# Privacy and consent

Only process recordings you have the right and necessary consent to use. Requirements differ by jurisdiction and context; this document is not legal advice.

Processing occurs in the user's Colab runtime or local environment. Model weights are downloaded from official providers. Diarizator does not upload audio to a proprietary diarization API. Source audio is excluded from result ZIPs by default, but a Colab runtime and its uploads remain under the user's account and provider controls. Review results before sharing because transcripts and speaker labels may reveal personal information.
